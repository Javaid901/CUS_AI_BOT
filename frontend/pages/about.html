About Cluster University

Established in 2016 under the Srinagar and Jammu Cluster Universities Act, created under RUSA.

History

Cluster University of Srinagar (CUS) was established in 2016 under the Srinagar and Jammu Cluster Universities Act, as a premier state university created under the Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan (RUSA). It integrates five prestigious constituent colleges of Srinagar into a single academic community.

University at a Glance

• 5 constituent colleges across Srinagar & South Kashmir
• UG, PG, professional and research programmes
• Choice-based credit system under NEP 2020
• On-premise AI Assistant for students & visitors
• Green, inclusive and research-driven campus
